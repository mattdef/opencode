## Loss Analysis

For your losing trades:

1. **Which step did you skip or rush?**
   - [ ] Weather (wrong market context)
   - [ ] News (traded during news)
   - [ ] Levels (poor zone selection)
   - [ ] Scenarios (no plan)
   - [ ] Confirmation (entered too early)
   - [ ] Execution (hesitated)
   - [ ] Risk mgmt (bad SL placement)

2. **Was it a bad process or bad luck?**
   - [ ] Bad process → Fix the step
   - [ ] Bad luck → Process was fine, move on

3. **Did you break any rules?**
   - [ ] Traded news
   - [ ] Entered without confirmation
   - [ ] Moved stop loss
   - [ ] Averaged down
   - [ ] Revenge traded
